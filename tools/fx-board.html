Element FX approval board: the §31 pixel FX atlas and the §32 element particle language

Everything is drawn from js/elfx.js. Each sheet is baked once as grey levels and tinted through one source-in cache. Positions use the closed-form models, so every frame is a pure function of time. Each particle is a single drawImage. The scene draws at 1x (one particle pixel is one scene pixel) and the board zooms it. The HUD draws on the 2x grid. The ground is the Vale's violet stone.

1 · Pixel FX atlas: slash arc, fire burst, frost hit (animated) + frame strips

Grey-level frames (Lumina FxSprites painters) tinted from the same ramps as the particles. The last frames fade through the 4×4 Bayer dissolve rows. Bottom row: the one slash arc grey sheet re-tinted with five other ramps, so a fire slash and a fire ember agree to the pixel.

2 · Element particle sets: all 9 hero elements + shatter + gloom (animated, 1x scene pixels, shown ×4)

Each cell shows the idle tier (ambient, rate per A2) at a training post, plus an event burst every 1.6 s. Beside it: the motif frames at ×8 in colour and in grey, with the motion model and life band. The minutes' approval set is slash, fire and frost (first row). The client asked for every element, so they are all shown.

3 · Lightning vs holy: the 16 px chip pair, colour and greyscale

The pair is told apart three ways. Shape: a zigzag versus a point that blinks into a cross. Motion: a strobe that stays in place versus a slow straight rise. Lifetime: 0.12–0.25 s versus 1.2–2.0 s. Below that: 8 frames of each at 16 px, then the 16 px chip strip of all 11 rows in greyscale.

4 · HUD mock (2x grid): skill chips with element particles, a WEAK number, a boss bar with element chips

These particles come from the UIP screen pool (ELFX.makePool(48), 2x sheets, so one particle pixel is a 2×2 HUD block). A slot that comes off cooldown flashes a white rim and sends out 4 motifs. A cast sends 3. Cooldown slots get nothing. The WEAK number has 3 motifs behind the text. Each weak chip on the boss bar emits 1 motif every 2 s, clipped below the HP fill. The hero status row emits 1 motif every 1.2 s from each slot's top edge.

5 · Status mock-ups on an enemy: the tint, overlay, pose, particles and over-head glyph for each tier

The layer order is B4: body, tint, overlay, particles, then the glyph. The glyph is 8×8 scene px, with a 1 px timer ring and a stack digit where it stacks. Tints and overlays are cached per sprite frame (ENEMY_ART tint keys plus 3 overlay bakers). Poses are draw offsets or held frames, never e.x.
