# Story and bosses

Terms: [../GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md). Boss rules, phases and fairness: [enemies-bosses.md](enemies-bosses.md). Zones: [world-brief.md](world-brief.md).

## Premise
**Morrow** was the first Dreamwarden. When his daughter **Aerin** died in the waking world, he refused to wake and
dreamed her back. The **Gloam** is the cost: his dream spreads and drowns the other Wardens' islands. The four Wardens
tried to wake him; he turned them into guardians of his sleep. **Pip** is the last light of the real Aerin's lantern and
the only one who remembers.

The hero, "Aerin", has no memory because she is three days old. **She is Morrow's dream of his daughter.** Every guardian
knows it; the player learns it in pieces.

**The twist (end of Act IV):** relighting the Shards does not wake Morrow; it wakes you. Each Shard makes Aerin more
real and Morrow weaker, and Pip has known since the Vale.

**Default ending:** at the Spire, Pip gives itself up to become the Dawn Burst; you wake Morrow; he chooses to let the
dream end, which means letting you end. Dawn in the Vale, Aerin fading gently, Pip rekindled as a spark. Bittersweet, 30 s.

**True ending (100%: every Fishing Log legendary, the Gloaming Below cleared, every lore tablet read, Pip's lantern
quest done):** you carry the Moonmaw's pearl into the finale; Morrow wakes and Aerin stays. The dream keeps one island.

**NG+:** "Morrow dreams again." Enemies x1.6, Mythic rolls, and Pip's lines change to show it remembers the last run.

## Voice rules (every line at most 12 words)
* Pip: short, warm, deflecting. ("Later. Ogre now.") Guardians: formal, tired. Morrow: gentle, never shouts.
* Elder Stone: riddling, speaks of itself in the third person.

## Optional areas and side quests
* **Drifting Isles:** the Wardens' graves. The sixth legendary belongs to the fifth Warden, **Sorrel**, who woke Morrow once and was exiled. Boss: Sorrel's Echo.
* **Starlit Shoals:** where the real Aerin drowned. The Moonmaw lives here; Mythic chance.
* **The Gloaming Below:** Morrow's buried nightmares, corrupted versions of all four guardians. Floor boss: the Unwaking Child (Aerin's shadow).
* Side quests: Vale, the Stone's lost lantern. Pinewood, a fox kit's mother (ogres). Ruins, the names on the pillars (reveals AERIN early for the attentive).
  Grotto, relight the fishermen's lanterns. Caldera, Kharzul's apprentice golem. Spire, Pip's three lanterns (powers the true ending).

## Bosses
Pinewood teaches dodging, the Ruins positioning, the Grotto adds the arena, the Caldera adds resource pressure, and the Spire adds
everything plus a phase that changes the rules. Every wind-up is at least 0.6 s. Bosses can kill in one telegraphed hit; there is no hit cap.

### Guardians
* **Old Bramblehorn** (Pinewood, L8): clearing arena with four root pillars; beast, nature; 2 phases (100 / 50%). Stomp (circle r3, 0.9 s wind-up),
  root-grab (line 7 that roots 1.5 s; dodge i-frames or the Root-Crown answer it), trunk-swipe cone. Phase 2 adds root-wall (the arena shrinks) and summons saplings. Stagger 5.
  Counter: Wildfire on the saplings. Drops Root-Crown.
* **The Gloam Warden** (Ruins, L14): cleave, slam, volley, charge, void rain, adds, enrage at 50%; phase 3 at 20%: pillars fall (arena shrinks), the volley becomes a spiral and the hall goes dark (move `gloam`: only the lantern's light is safe). Code id `gloamwarden` (the legacy M2a fight stays `boss`).
  Ethereal: resists physical, weak holy. The Lantern Plate answers the volley. Counter: Overload. Drops Warden's Lantern Plate.
* **Lady Mirelle** (Grotto, L20): flooded ring arena; the water rises in phase 2 (slow zone on the outer ring). Frost; resists frost, weak lightning.
  Tidal wave (wide sweeping line, 1.3 s), pull (circle r6), icicle rain (6 circles), freeze-beam (rotating line). 60%: drowned adds; 25%: the arena halves.
  Counter: Conduct (everything is wet). Drops Tide-Edge.
* **Kharzul the Cinder Colossus** (Caldera, L26): forge platform; the lava floor spreads each phase (boots, Cinderstride, Ruby resist). Armoured fire golem, immune Burn, weak frost.
  Lava-ring, hammer-slam (circle r4, 1.1 s wind-up, then a stagger window), magma-spit arc, forge-stomp (3 lines). 55%: floor tiles turn to lava in a pattern;
  20%: enrage, rings every 4 s. Stagger 8, break 2.5 s. Counter: Shatter, then Rupture. Drops Cinderstride Boots.
* **Morrow, the Unwaking King** (Spire, L34): immune gloom, weak holy.
  * Phase 1 Sword-Knight: slash combos (cone x3), charge, dawn-wave line; can be staggered.
  * Phase 2 Dream-Warp: void tiles vanish (arena shrink pattern), blink-behind, mirror image (two Morrows, one real; holy reveals it), Dread aura.
  * Phase 3 The Unwaking: giant form, starfall (double void rain), gaze-beam.
  * Pip's sacrifice, mechanically: in phase 3 Dawn Burst takes the ultimate slot (the bar starts full and refills only from reactions).
    Pip's light is the only safe zone during starfall and moves each cast: stand in it, then spend it. Drops Morrowbane (Mythic).
* Optional: **Sorrel's Echo** (Drifting Isles, L38, lightning; mirrors the player's own skills; drops Sorrel's Vow, which gives the boots skill a second charge)
  and **the Unwaking Child** (Gloaming Below, L40, gloom; three stolen guardian moves per phase; Mythic chance).

## BOSS_MOVES library
Bosses are data (`bosses.js`) built from one move library. Common parameters: `wind` (telegraph s), `dmg`, `el`, `stagger`, `col`.
* cone (arc, r); circle (r, at self or player); line (len, w); charge (len, speed, hitOnce); volley (n, rings, spiral, speed); rain (count, r, spread);
  ring (expanding r0 to r1, speed); beam (len, rotate, dur); pull (r, force); summon (type, n, pattern); zone (lava, void or slow; cells, life); arenaShrink (pattern id);
  blink (behind or random); mirror (n images); shield (arc, dur); vanish (dur); roar (banner, dur); sequence (list, gaps); random (weights).
* Phase schema: `phases: [{ hpBelow, moves: { name: weight }, onEnter: [actions] }]` plus `staggerMax` and `breakOpen`. The Vigils share HP through a `linked` flag.
* Dialogue lives in a `DIALOGUE` table. Risks: do not over-explain the twist; `mirror` and `arenaShrink` can break pathing on generated arenas;
  every boss ships with one screenshot per telegraph.
