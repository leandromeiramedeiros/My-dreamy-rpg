# Balance

Terms: [../GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md). Items and caps: [items.md](items.md). Enemies and bosses: [enemies-bosses.md](enemies-bosses.md).
Loadout parity targets: [weapons-and-skills.md](weapons-and-skills.md).

**Client ruling 2026-10-23: the client tunes the numbers** (in Godot, editor-first authoring). The studio builds assets and logic and
no longer tunes to these targets: they are reference values, and the sims and bots are report-only tools (a crash or a
non-completable arena still fails). Balance red flags never block a ship.

## Framework
`tools/balsim.js` runs the real SKILLS and damage maths headless with a scripted rotation against each zone's reference enemy and boss,
for reference, random and optimal builds. Sheets are generated by `tools/balance/sheets.js`.
* Loadout loop: hand x offhand x suit search, 4 kits per class pair, +-15% parity at equal tier, no kit above a 60% pick rate.
* Parity: weapon variants +-15% DPS at equal tier; utility 0.8 to 1.2x of the class mean.
  A variant status bonus is +15% `statusChance`, additive.

## Item power points (1 pp = 1% DPS or eHP)
| Source | pp |
|---|---|
| Tier upgrade | 40 (weapons) / 25 (armour) |
| Rarity step | 15 |
| Affix T1 / T2 / T3 | 4 / 8 / 13 |
| Temper | +4 to 5 |
| Gems by grade | 4 / 8 / 14 |
| Implicit | 5 |
| Unique rule | 15 to 25 in-build, 5 or less outside |

* Trinkets are at most 25% of gear power. An optimal build beats random by about 1.6x DPS and 1.4x eHP; the CI band is 1.3 to 2.2x.
* Caps (`CAPS`): cdr 40, crit 60, haste 50, gear power 150, reaction bonus 50, Mark cap 5, potions 5.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Pinewood | 110 to 220 | 1.1k to 1.9k | 2.5 to 3.5 s | 12 s | 45 to 90 |
| Ruins | 220 to 420 | 1.9k to 3.0k | 3 to 4 s | 14 s | 90 to 160 |
| Grotto | 420 to 700 | 3.0k to 4.4k | 3 to 4 s | 15 s | 160 to 250 |
| Caldera | 700 to 1.1k | 4.4k to 6.2k | 3.5 to 4.5 s | 16 s | 250 to 370 |
| Spire | 1.1k to 1.6k | 6.2k to 8.5k | 4 to 5 s | 18 s | 370 to 520 |

* Common TTK stays 2.5 to 5 s. Standing in 3 commons, the hero dies in 10 to 14 s.
* `enemyHpMul` is about 1.08 per level; gear tier steps are about 45%.
* Boss length: mini 60 to 90 s, guardian 150 to 240 s, Morrow about 300 s. Boss HP = length x reference DPS x uptime (0.7 mini, 0.6 guardian).
* A telegraphed big hit takes 35 to 45% of eHP; chip damage 8 to 12%.

## CI red flags
Each fails the gate.
* Common TTK under 1.5 s or over 7 s. Mini-boss under 45 s or over 120 s. Guardian under 100 s or over 300 s.
* Optimal over random outside 1.3 to 2.2x. Reaction share above 40%. An ultimate faster than every 15 s.
* Any stat sum over its cap. Dust sinks larger than income. More than 2 potions per room. Hero level at Morrow outside 34 to 40.
* Element red flags: [elements-statuses.md](elements-statuses.md). Pacing flags: [enemies-bosses.md](enemies-bosses.md).
* Suit rules: eHP within +-10% per class, i-frame uptime at most 50%, poise at most 75% ([armour-suits.md](armour-suits.md)).
* Hush adds at most 20% boss-fight DPS; Echo adds 25 to 30% for an element-focused build ([combat-feel.md](combat-feel.md)).

## Ship gate
`node tools/check.js` runs: smoke (balsim + save-migrate), `tools/play.js`, `tools/arena-route.js`, `tools/validate-ids.js` (including the data-export round trip),
`tools/perf.js` and `tools/qa/run-arenas.js kbm|pad|touch`. Arenas: [arenas.md](arenas.md).
Perf budgets, measured on the `stress` arena: phone p95 at most 20.0 ms, desktop at most 16.7 ms.
Boss fight times are judged with the in-game reference tier at the boss's level (Act II: T2 at L12 to L14), never a higher band. The Bot fights each `pack-*` and `boss-*` arena with reference gear per band; `--god` stays blocker-only.
