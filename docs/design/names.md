# Names appendix: tier and Mythic names

Canonical terms live in [../GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md). Tiers T1 to T5 gate at reqLvl 1/8/16/24/32; Mythic at 36 (see [items.md](items.md)).
The look escalates by tier: grey, ornate, sun- or moon-etched, glowing, made of light ([visual-bible.md](visual-bible.md)).
Each story name is used once. Mythic names are story names, not tier steps.
