# Story & bosses (authoritative; supersedes DESIGN.md §2 and the boss lines of §7)

## Premise
**Morrow** was the first Dreamwarden. When his daughter **Aerin** died in the waking world, he refused to wake and
dreamed her back. The **Gloam** is the cost: his dream spreads and drowns the other Wardens' islands. The four Wardens
tried to wake him; he turned them into guardians of his sleep. **Pip** is the last light of the real Aerin's lantern and
the only one who remembers.

The hero, "Aerin", has no memory because she is three days old. **She is Morrow's dream of his daughter.** Every guardian
knows it; the player learns it in pieces.

**The twist (end of Act IV):** relighting the Shards does not wake Morrow; it wakes *you*. Each Shard makes Aerin more
real and Morrow weaker, and Pip has known since the Vale.

**Default ending:** at the Spire, Pip gives itself up to become the Dawn Burst; you wake Morrow; he chooses to let the
dream end, which means letting you end. Dawn in the Vale, Aerin fading gently, Pip rekindled as a spark. Bittersweet, 30 s.

**True ending (100 %: every Fishing Log legendary, the Gloaming Below cleared, every lore tablet read, Pip's lantern
quest done):** you carry the **Moonmaw's pearl** into the finale; Morrow wakes and Aerin stays. The dream keeps one island.

**NG+:** "Morrow dreams again." Enemies ×1.6, Mythic rolls, and Pip's lines change to show it remembers the last run.

## Voice rules (every line ≤ 12 words)
* **Pip:** short, warm, deflecting. ("Later. Ogre now.")
* **Guardians:** formal, tired.
* **Morrow:** gentle, never shouts.
* **Elder Stone:** riddling, speaks of itself in the third person.

## Optional areas and side quests
* **Drifting Isles:** the Wardens' graves. The sixth legendary belongs to the fifth Warden, **Sorrel**, who woke Morrow
  once and was exiled. Boss: Sorrel's Echo.
* **Starlit Shoals:** where the real Aerin drowned. The Moonmaw lives here; Mythic chance.
* **The Gloaming Below:** Morrow's buried nightmares: corrupted versions of all four guardians. Floor boss: the Unwaking
  Child (Aerin's shadow).
* Side quests:
  * Vale: the Stone's lost lantern (rewards the first Dream Tome).
  * Pinewood: a fox kit's mother (ogres).
  * Ruins: the names on the pillars (reveals AERIN early for attentive players).
  * Grotto: relight the fishermen's lanterns.
  * Caldera: Kharzul's apprentice golem.
  * Spire: Pip's three lanterns (powers the true ending).

## Bosses
Escalation: Pinewood teaches dodging, the Ruins positioning, the Grotto adds the arena, the Caldera adds resource
pressure, and the Spire adds everything plus a phase that changes the rules. Fairness rules: every wind-up ≥ 0.6 s;
every big hit leaves ≥ 1.2 s open to punish.

### Guardians
* **Old Bramblehorn** (Pinewood, L8) — clearing arena with four root pillars; beast, nature; 2 phases (100 / 50 %).
  Stomp (circle r3, 0.9 s wind-up, 1.5 s open), root-grab (line 7 that roots 1.5 s — *the mechanic*: dodge i-frames or
  the Root-Crown), trunk-swipe cone; phase 2 adds root-wall (the arena shrinks) and summons saplings. Stagger 5.
  Counter: Wildfire on the saplings. Drops **Root-Crown**.
* **The Gloam Warden** (Ruins, L14) — the existing fight ported to the move library: cleave, slam, volley, charge,
  void rain, enrage at 50 %, adds; new phase 3 at 20 %: pillars fall (arena shrinks) and the volley becomes a spiral.
  Ethereal: resists physical, weak holy. Mechanic: Bulwark reflects the volley (Lantern Plate). Counter: Overload
  (Static Field → Barrage). Drops **Warden's Lantern Plate**.
* **Lady Mirelle** (Grotto, L20) — flooded ring arena; the water rises in phase 2 (slow zone on the outer ring). Frost;
  resists frost, weak lightning. Tidal wave (wide sweeping line, 1.3 s: Blink or stand on the pillar), pull (circle r6 —
  Dreamveil / stand firm), icicle rain (6 circles), freeze-beam (rotating line). 60 %: drowned adds; 25 %: the arena
  halves. Counter: Conduct (everything is wet). Drops **Tide-Edge**.
* **Kharzul the Cinder Colossus** (Caldera, L26) — forge platform; the lava floor spreads each phase (boots,
  Cinderstride, Ruby resist). Armoured fire golem, immune Burn, weak frost. Lava-ring, hammer-slam (circle r4, 1.1 s,
  2 s open = the stagger window), magma-spit arc, forge-stomp (3 lines). 55 %: floor tiles turn to lava in a pattern;
  20 %: enrage, rings every 4 s. Stagger 8, break 2.5 s. Counter: Shatter, then Rupture. Drops **Cinderstride Boots**.
* **Morrow, the Unwaking King** (Spire, L34) — immune gloom, weak holy.
  * Phase 1 *Sword-Knight* (100–65 %): slash combos (cone ×3), charge, dawn-wave line; can be staggered.
  * Phase 2 *Dream-Warp* (65–30 %): void tiles vanish (arena shrink pattern), blink-behind, mirror image (two Morrows,
    one real — holy reveals it), Dread aura.
  * Phase 3 *The Unwaking* (30–0 %): giant form, starfall (double void rain), gaze-beam.
  * **Pip's sacrifice, mechanically:** at phase 3 the R slot becomes **Dawn Burst** (bar starts full; it refills only
    from reactions). Pip's light is the only safe zone during starfall and moves each cast: stand in Pip's light, then
    spend it. Drops **Morrowbane** (Mythic).
* Optional: **Sorrel's Echo** (Drifting Isles, L38, lightning; mirrors the player's own skill set; drops **Sorrel's Vow**,
  which gives the boots skill a second charge) and **the Unwaking Child** (Gloaming Below, L40, gloom; three stolen
  guardian moves per phase; Mythic chance).

## BOSS_MOVES library (Opus builds once; bosses are then data in `world.js`)
Common parameters: `wind` (telegraph s), `open` (punish s), `dmg`, `el`, `stagger`, `col`.
* **cone** (arc, r) · **circle** (r, at: self | player) · **line** (len, w) · **charge** (len, speed, hitOnce)
* **volley** (n, rings, spiral, speed) · **rain** (count, r, spread, at player) · **ring** (expanding r0→r1, speed)
* **beam** (len, rotate °/s, dur) · **pull** (r, force, then move) · **summon** (type, n, pattern)
* **zone** (kind lava | void | slow, cells pattern, life) · **arenaShrink** (pattern id) · **blink** (behind | random)
* **mirror** (n images) · **shield** (arc, dur) · **vanish** (dur, then move) · **roar** (banner, dur)
* **sequence** (list, gaps) · **random** (weights)

Phase schema: `phases: [{ hpBelow, moves: { name: weight }, onEnter: [actions] }]` plus `staggerMax`, `breakOpen`.
Two bosses sharing HP (the Vigils) use a `linked` flag. Acceptance test: the Gloam Warden port plays like today's fight.
