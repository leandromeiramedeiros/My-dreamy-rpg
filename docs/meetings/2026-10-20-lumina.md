## 0. The frame that decides everything

1. **M3 is full.** The queue is Echo (§30) → opening (§28) → world art G2–G3 (§29) → phone perf (§26.4) → QA → ship.
   Nothing from Lumina may add a lane to M3. "Adopt now" therefore means **ride a lane that is already running or queued,
   at near-zero extra days** (a brief or a reference file handed to the agent who owns that lane). Everything that needs
   `game.js`/`render.js` time of its own is M4.
2. **Lumina is a different game in a different medium.** Golden-hour village, tilt-shift, running clock, weather,
   three-member party, DOM UI, three.js. We are a violet dreamy floating-islands action RPG on Canvas 2D, one hero,
   phone-first. We take *craft* (ramps, noise rules, timing tables, AI fairness rules), not *signature* (tilt-shift
   bands, gold/navy UI, clock, weather, party), unless the client chooses otherwise in §6.
3. **Three lift classes, so nobody budgets a lift that is really a port:**
   * **COPY** (cheaper to copy, credit in `docs/CREDITS.md`): zero-import pure JS or data. `Palette.js`,
     `PixelCanvas.js` (minus 2 THREE functions), `Textures.js` painters, `StoryCondition.js`, `CyclicPchip` +
     `DEFAULT_KEYFRAMES`, `WeatherLook.js`, `WaterShore.bakeShore`, `ResolutionGovernor` logic, `CombatMusic.js` timers,
     `flickerNoise`, `FxSprites.js` frame painters, `CombatFx` `EFFECTS` table, `I18n.js`. Each still needs `import/export`
     → classic-script globals (a 10-minute mechanical pass per file) and a smoke run.
   * **RE-TYPE** (data tables typed into our format, credited): frame windows, shake table, hitstop table, telegraph
     rules, token budgets, Cinderheart constants, particle presets, pause tables. Not cheaper to "copy"; it is design
     work with a reference.
   * **REWRITE** (ours from the idea): anything on three.js, GLSL, Sprite3D, DOM or their Game/Npc/walk-grid.
4. **Licence** (see §5): the client owns the decision and has accepted the risk; the day estimates below assume copying.
   Director's proposed process rule, **pending D1**: COPY-class files go in only after one written message from the author
   is on file; RE-TYPE and REWRITE proceed now.
5. **Evidence from our own shots.** `strip-echo.png` and `strip-2-heavy-hit.png` show the hero, slash and Echo whiting out
   the target (the bloom bright-pass eats the fight). Lumina learnt the same lesson (their COMBAT-15/16: celebration
   colours *at* the bloom threshold, big white frames cut 40 %). `pinewood_4_ford_creek.png` "after" already shows G1 is
   landing the ground grammar; the trees and density (G2) are the visible gap, exactly where Lumina's rules are best.

## 4. What Lumina proves we should change in our own plans

1. **§29 G2 is where the look is won, not G1.** The compare strips show G1's ground grammar landing; what still separates
   us from `cinderwatch-ruins-fight` is tree volume and clutter density. Fold items #2–#4 into G2's brief and keep its
   5-day estimate; do not let G3 (set pieces) start before G2's density shows on the 6 acceptance shots.
2. **Our bloom is fighting our fights.** The feel and echo strips white out the target. Change §26.4 step 4 from "bloom at
   quarter resolution" to "**emitter-only bloom at quarter resolution, VFX at the threshold**", and add to §27's companion
   signals: the enemy flash is a sprite tint, never a bloom contributor.
3. **§27 should state the victim jitter** (draw offset, ±1 px, alternate frames) as part of the target-only freeze.
4. **§21.2 behaviour parts need a fairness grammar, not just verbs.** Add telegraph minimums (#14), poise per kind (#12)
   and the token budget (#13) as *global* rules of the bestiary, so the 28 commons get fairness by default.
5. **§15.3 stamina:** "winded, never refused" (recovery ×1.35 at 0 SP) beats our hard "Not enough stamina" refusal for
   feel and for touch. Client decision D4.
6. **§26.1 cunning forms** should include environment bait (brazier/pillar stun), the anti-flank trigger and the
   capped exposed kneel as named forms in the BOSS_MOVES library; the arena should send every other enemy home and seal.
7. **§26.2 gear loop** benefits from the pity heart and magnetised drops (#15); farming elite pockets must not feel like
   walking over coins.
8. **CONTROLS.md** has no lock-on. Lumina's tap-to-cycle / hold-to-release with 30 % camera focus (45 % on bosses, pull
   back to frame both) is cheap in 2D and matters for pad and touch boss fights. Client decision D5.
9. **Dialogue** (§2 / STORY.md delivery): add portraits from our own sheets and punctuation pauses; the first choice is
   always non-committal. Cheap, visible, and the client is visual.
10. **The perf lane needs the phone reading first.** Every per-frame item above (#5, #8, #9, tilt-shift) is gated on
    `?perf=1` from a real device; stop estimating against the 4× throttle number.

## 5. Risks

* **Licence.** All three surveys independently found that the repo says "A license has not been selected yet", with no
  LICENSE file and no `license` field. The client has accepted this and owns it. Director process: (a) keep one written
  message from the author (date, scope: code + data, attribution terms) in `docs/CREDITS.md` before any COPY-class file
  is committed; (b) RE-TYPE and REWRITE proceed now and are credited as "inspired by"; (c) credit per file, with the
  Lumina path, in `docs/CREDITS.md` (does not exist yet; created on the first lift). If the message never arrives, the
  COPY items in §1 become REWRITE at roughly +50 % days; nothing on the plan depends on copying.
* **Identity dilution.** Lumina's screenshots are seductive and the client is visual. The risk is the game turning into a
  golden-hour village sim with a violet filter. Guardrails: no clock, no weather, no gold UI, tilt-shift only by client
  choice behind a setting, moods re-coloured to our ramps, and every adopted look judged on the 6 §29 acceptance shots.
* **Name and theme collision.** Lumina has *Emberfall* village, *The Caldera*, *Cinderheart* the golem with braziers and
  magma pools. We have *Emberfall Caldera* and *Kharzul the Cinder Colossus*. Lifting Cinderheart's kit into Kharzul
  reads as the same boss, credit line or not. Rule: spread the 8 mechanics across different bosses; Kharzul keeps at
  most two of them, and never the brazier-charge.
* **Perf.** The phone p95 is already over budget (53–66 ms under 4× throttle vs 20 ms). Every per-frame item is gated on
  the real-device reading; chunk-build items must fit G1's +40 % raster budget; ambient particles stay under `PART.cap`.
  Emitter-only bloom is the one item expected to *reduce* cost.
* **ES-module/three.js code into classic scripts.** Each COPY file needs imports/exports removed, `THREE.Color` → arrays,
  60 Hz frames → our fixed step, world units → tiles (1 u ≈ 1 tile, ×16 px for sizes), Y-up 3D → iso. Rule: one agent
  ports one file at a time behind a smoke run; no file lands with a dangling `import`. Add each new `js/*.js` to both
  `index.html` and `editor.html` (DESIGN §0). Prefer pasting helpers into existing files (`util.js`, `art.js`, `maps.js`)
  over new files.
* **Scope creep in M3.** Any §1A item that slips a G2/G3 or perf date drops to M4 the same day.
* **Balance.** Poise, rear/exposed crit and tokens change TTK; balsim red flags (§20.7) gate them. Perfect dodge's
  enemy-only slow-mo must not stack with Echo's replay timing.
