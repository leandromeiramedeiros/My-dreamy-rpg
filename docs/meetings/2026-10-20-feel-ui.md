## Facts from the code that shaped every answer
F1. There is no death state. killEnemy() sets e.dead = true and updateEnemies() filters it out the same step. Death today
    = a 24-particle burst + a ring. ENEMY_ART ships a 3-frame `death` anim per key that nothing plays; legacy sprites
    (slime, hound, ogre, drawn at 1.5x) have no death frames at all.
F2. hitstop() is Math.max, never additive; it already cannot stack. It is only called from hitCone (0.035 s, melee cones),
    four heavy skills in data.js (0.06-0.1), boss moves and boss death (0.25). hitArea, hitLine, projectiles and burn
    ticks never call it: a bow or staff hero feels no hitlag at all today.
F3. Loop order (main.js 172-174): slowmo scales rdt first, then `if (G.hitstopT > 0) { G.hitstopT -= rdt; dt = 0 }`.
    Two consequences: 0.035 s freezes 3 steps not 2; under slowmo (x0.35) the boss-death 0.25 s hitstop is ~0.7 s of real
    time. Hitstop zeroes dt for everything (hero and enemies) while rdt still drives particles, texts, shake, camera.
F4. Knockback: v0 = o.knock * 9 * knockK tiles/s, decays pow(0.002, dt) => travel ~ v0 / 6.2 tiles. A projectile's
    default knock 0.12 travels ~0.17 tiles; a 0.5 heavy ~0.73 tiles. Swarm members have knockK 0.25.
F5. e.dead is the key for enemyAwake, arena all_enemies_dead, hit tests, nameplates and the kill filter. A lingering
    "dying" enemy would delay gates and triggers.
F6. Particles: pooled, cap 600, budget 0.35..1 adaptive on frame time; hit burst = 5, kill burst = 24, boss kill 120.
F7. Damage numbers already merge per enemy within 80 ms (dmgText). Crit/weak = heading size gold/yellow; everything else
    small; size does not follow hit power.
F8. The render y-sort already carries a 'ghost' effect kind (silhouette sprite at x,y,z) drawn in depth order; a corpse
    record can be a sibling of it.
F9. sfx(id, pos, opts) takes pan/gain/at; the synth has detune per note, but no per-call pitch option is exposed yet.
F10. Settings: 'hitstop' is a toggle; schema supports 'choice'; 'flashReduce' caps bloom/light flash only (not sprite flash).
F11. Phone p95 ~25 ms vs 20 ms budget (PRODUCTION.md 176 notes 53-66 ms under 4x throttle). Any new per-frame work must be
    O(small constant) with no allocations.

## Meeting minutes

### Q1 Ragdoll
GD: "Ragdoll" in a 2x pixel top-down game means one thing to the player: the body leaves the spot where it was hit, in
the direction I hit it, with weight. They do not see joints. Today enemies pop into lilac motes the same frame they die
(F1), which is tonally fine but reads as weightless and the player never gets the "I sent it flying" beat. We ship a
death fling. Nothing else is visible at 24 px.
PP: Verlet bodies are out: per-part sprites, constraints, and a sort per part; on a phone already 5 ms over budget that is
the first thing to cut. Gibs need per-type art splits, that is a content lane we do not have. A fling is a particle with a
sprite: x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, bounce, life. We already run that math 600 times a frame for motes. Cost is ~12 drawImage per
frame at the cap. The one thing we must not do is ctx.rotate on a pixel sprite: it breaks the 2x grid and smears.
GPD: Readability first. A dead body that still looks like a live enemy for half a second is a liability in a pack fight:
the player wastes a swing on it or dodges a ghost. So the corpse must read dead instantly: it leaves the ground (z arc),
it is the hit/death frame not the idle one, it is 70% alpha, and it never gets a nameplate. Also the fling must not carry
loot away: drops spawn at the kill point as now. And no corpses on DoT kills (burn): a thing that slumps from a tick
should not launch.
GD (tone): STORY.md is gentle melancholy. The body does not splat; it arcs, lands with a squash, and dissolves into the
motes that already exist. We move half the existing death burst from the kill point to the landing point. That is a
dream fading, not a gore pile.
Disagreement: GD wanted bosses to fling too ("the big one going down is the moment"). GPD and PP: no. A 2-3x sprite
flying over the arena hides the loot drop and the phase-clear read, and we already have slowmo + 120 motes for bosses.
Resolved: bosses hold a death pose (ENEMY_ART death frame 0, legacy: hit silhouette) for 1.2 s with staged bursts, then
dissolve. The slowmo stays. The hitstop-under-slowmo bug (F3) is fixed as part of this.
Big non-lethal hits: GPD wants the "tumble" for hits >= 30% of max HP on non-bosses: the existing knockback plus a small
z hop and a 0.3 s stun with squash on landing. That is the "knockback-tumble" option and it is 10 lines on top of what
exists. Swarm members (knockK 0.25) never tumble or fling; they squash-pop.

### Q2 Hitlag
GD: The ask is already approved polish: DESIGN §16.1 item 1 says "hitstop scaled by damage %". The thing the player
notices is not the freeze length, it is that a 5% hit and a 40% hit feel different. Today everything melee is 3 frames
and everything ranged is 0 (F2). That is the real gap.
PP: Two tiers, because they cost different things. (a) Target-only freeze: e.hitFreeze steps, checked at the top of the
enemy update (skip AI, move, anim) and in frame selection. Costs nothing, adds zero input latency, so it can run on every
hit including projectiles. (b) Global freeze (existing hitstop, dt = 0): freezes the hero too, so on touch it is felt as
latency. Keep it for crits, kills and heavy hits, cap it hard. Also: specify in frames, not seconds; today 0.035 s = 3
steps because of the loop order (F3). Fix the loop to count hitstop down in unscaled STEP and check before subtracting.
GPD: Formula lives in damageEnemy, once, so every weapon path gets it; delete the hard-coded hitstop(0.035) in hitCone.
Scale on r = dmg / e.maxHp, not on raw damage, so gear growth is felt as "enemies die faster and each hit lands harder",
not as one constant. Crit adds, weight adds, DoT ticks get nothing. For AoE: one global call per cast, from the largest
r among the targets, never the sum; target-only freezes stay per target. hitstop is already Math.max (F2) so stacking
across overlapping casts is already clamped.
Disagreement: GD pushed for 8-10 global frames on kills ("let it ring"). PP: 10 frames is 167 ms; a touch player doing
hold-attack notices every one of those as the hero going dead. GPD: and in a pack a kill every 0.3 s would mean the game
is frozen a third of the time. Resolved: global kill freeze 3 frames, crit 3, heavy (r >= 0.25) 4, r >= 0.5 or ultimate
6, boss BREAK 8; plus a budget of 12 global frames per rolling 0.5 s, overflow goes to target-only.
Accessibility: the existing toggle gates only hitstop(). Change it to a choice Off / Light / Full (schema supports
'choice', F10). Off = no global freeze, target-only 2 frames stays (it is just animation). Everything else that signals
power (number size, knockback, flash, pitch, particles) must still scale when hitstop is Off. flashReduce caps the sprite
flash at today's 0.1 s.
Other signals, agreed set: damage number size by r (F7 only sizes crit/weak today), flash length by r, knockback by r,
hit-burst count by r, sfx pitch down with r (needs opts.pitch, F9). Cut: camera zoom punch (grid smear), per-hit colour
grading, slowmo on non-boss kills.

legendary tooltip (desktop) looks like a shipped game. The title screen is the weakest shot: three save-slot boxes with

## Agreed recommendations

### R1 Death fling + tumble (Q1)
What ships: "corpse" records, not a ragdoll. Verlet bodies: cut. Gibs: cut. Sprite rotation: forbidden.

Data: G.corpses, pooled like PART (no per-kill allocation), cap CORPSE.cap = PHONE ? 8 : 12; when full, the oldest is
dissolved immediately. Record: { x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, face, spr (canvas) or artKey + frame list, t, life, sq (squash),
bounced, col (blood), scale }.

Spawn in killEnemy(e) (game.js ~935), before the burst, for every non-boss, non-dummy, non-under, non-swarm-member enemy,
when the killing hit was not a DoT:
  r  = clamp(dmgOfKillingHit / e.maxHp, 0, 1)          (pass dmg from damageEnemy into killEnemy(e, dmg, o))
  dir = unit vector from the hit source (o.fx, o.fy, else player) to the enemy (same as the knockback block)
  speed = 2.5 + 5 * r   tiles/s     (r = 0.1 -> 3 t/s -> ~0.5 tile travel with pow(0.002) decay; r = 1 -> ~1.2 tiles)
  vz = 3 + 5 * r        (particle gravity 9 => airtime 0.67-1.8 s; clamp life so the body lands by 0.6 s: use grav 14)
  life = 0.55 s + 0.25 s fade; bounce once: on z < 0, vz *= -0.35, vx,vy *= 0.5, sq = 0.3 for 0.12 s
  DoT kill or r < 0.08: slump instead: speed 0, vz 0, sq 0.25, life 0.45 s (still a corpse so the death frames play)
  Swarm member: no corpse; keep today's burst plus e.squash-style pop (parts.js 292 pattern).
Burst split: of the 24 kill particles, 8 at the kill point now, 16 at the landing point when the corpse's life ends.
Art: ENEMY_ART enemies play death frames 0,1,2 over life (frame = floor(3 * t / life)); legacy sprites draw the hit
silhouette (silOf(img, blood colour at 60% alpha)) with the landing squash (w * 1.3, h * 0.6 for the squash window).
Alpha 0.7 while airborne, linear to 0 across the fade. Flying enemies (tags 'flying') use vz = 1 and fall from their hover
offset. Face = e.face flipped to the fling direction.
Render: render.js y-sort list (286-303) gets `{ kind: 'corpse', c, key: c.x + c.y + 0.005 }`; drawItem handles it next
to 'ghost' (615). Never a nameplate (it is not in G.enemies), never lights. Loot, XP, kill counters, triggers unchanged
and immediate (F5 respected: e.dead stays true the same step).
Big non-lethal hits ("tumble"): in damageEnemy after the knockback block (line ~786), for non-boss, non-dummy, non-swarm,
r >= 0.3 and not o.dot: e.stunT = max(e.stunT, 0.3); e.tumbleZ = 2.5 (a z hop handled next to the knockback integration
at 1027: e.z += vz*dt, grav 12, squash 0.3 on landing). Knockback for that hit x1.5.
Bosses: no fling. On killEnemy for e.boss: hold a death pose for 1.2 s (ENEMY_ART death frame 0 or hit silhouette) with 3
bursts at 0, 0.5, 1.0 s (40 each, same 120 total), then dissolve. Keep shake(14) and slowmo 1.4. Fix F3 so the 0.25 s
hitstop is 0.25 s real, not 0.7.
Cost: 12 drawImage + 12 integrations per frame worst case: under 0.2 ms on phone. No new js file (DESIGN §0): corpses
live in game.js next to PART; drawing in render.js.
Files: js/game.js (killEnemy, damageEnemy, updateEnemies -> updateCorpses, PART-style pool), js/render.js (list +
drawItem), js/enemyart.js (nothing new; death anim already exists), js/main.js (reset G.corpses on enterMap/respawn).
Milestone: M3 (feel item on the current roster). 1.5 agent-days (1 corpse + fling, 0.25 tumble, 0.25 boss pose).

### R2 Hitlag scaled by power (Q2)
Single site: damageEnemy (game.js 739-801), after dmg is final and before killEnemy. Skipped when o.dot, e.dummy keeps
target-only only. Remove hitstop(0.035) from hitCone (824); leave data.js heavy-skill calls, they become the `w` weight
instead (see below) or stay as explicit overrides via Math.max.

  r  = clamp(dmg / e.maxHp, 0, 1)                   (for bosses use the current phase bar's max, §26.1)
  w  = skill weight: basic 1.0; light skill 1.0; heavy skill 1.5; ultimate 2.0   (SKILLS[sk].weight, default 1)
  Target-only freeze (new, every hit, including projectiles and hitArea):
     Ft = clamp(round((2 + 6 * r) * w), 2, 8) steps   -> e.hitFreeze = max(e.hitFreeze, Ft)
     In updateEnemies: if (e.hitFreeze > 0) { e.hitFreeze--; (flash/anim timers still tick); skip move/AI/knockback; continue }
     drawEnemy: while hitFreeze > 0 show the hit frame (already: e.flash > 0).
  Global freeze (existing hitstop(), hero included), in steps:
     Fg = 0
     crit            -> 3
     kill (non-boss) -> 3
     r >= 0.25       -> 4
     r >= 0.5 or ultimate hit -> 6
     boss BREAK (bossBreak) -> 8
     take the max of the applicable rows, multiply by nothing else. hitstop(Fg) stays Math.max (no stacking).
  Budget: G.hitstopSpent decays 24 steps per second; a global request is granted only while spent < 12 steps per
     rolling 0.5 s; otherwise it is converted to target-only (Ft = max(Ft, Fg)).
  Loop fix (main.js 172-174): count hitstop in unscaled steps: `if (G.hitstopF > 0) { G.hitstopF--; dt = 0; }` before
     the slowmo scaling; hitstop(frames) stores an integer. This also fixes the 0.7 s boss freeze (F3).
AoE / multi-hit rules: hitCone / hitArea / hitLine compute one global Fg from the max r across targets, once per cast
(they already call hitstop once; keep that shape). Per-target Ft still applies to each enemy. Projectile pierce: Fg
only on the first enemy hit per projectile; Ft on all. Burn ticks: nothing. Shake: shake(1.5 + 4 * r) per cast, max once
per cast, capped at 6 for non-boss hits (shakeMul() already applies the slider). Particle cap per cast: 5 + 8r per target,
but at most 24 hit particles per cast; beyond 4 targets, 2 per target (burst() already honours PART.budget).
Touch latency: target-only costs 0 ms. Global max 6 steps = 100 ms, and inputs are still read during hitstop (handleInput
runs on every step; dodgeBuf buffers the dodge). Keep the 16-step cap on pads and touch identical: no platform forks.
Accessibility: settings.js 'hitstop' becomes a choice: off / light / full (migrate boolean true->full, false->off).
  off  : Fg = 0 always; Ft = 2 (animation only)
  light: Fg capped at 3; Ft as computed
  full : as above
  flashReduce: caps e.flash at 0.1 s (today's value) and disables the boss-break white frame.
Companion signals (all scale on the same r, all independent of the hitstop setting):
  damage number size (hud.js drawDamageNumbers): r < 0.05 small; 0.05-0.2 body; >= 0.2 heading. crit stays gold heading,
     weak yellow heading. Carry r in the dmgText o (o.r) so merged numbers use the max r.
  enemy flash: e.flash = 0.1 + 0.1 * r (max 0.2; flashReduce -> 0.1).
  knockback: o.knock * (0.7 + 0.6 * r) (F4: a 0.12 arrow travels 0.12-0.22 tiles, a 0.5 heavy 0.5-1.0 tiles). Bosses: unchanged (none).
  hit burst: 5 + 8 * r particles (max 12 single target).
  sound: add opts.pitch (cents) to sfx(); 'hit' plays at -100 * (r * 6) cents clamped to [-600, 0]: heavier = lower.
     Crit uses the existing variant rotation plus +0 cents. One new line in audio.js's note path (detune exists, F9).
Cut: camera zoom punch, slowmo on common kills, per-hit vignette, additive hitstop, per-target global freeze in AoE.
Files: js/game.js (damageEnemy, hitCone, updateEnemies, hitstop()), js/main.js (loop), js/render.js (drawEnemy frame),
js/hud.js (drawDamageNumbers), js/settings.js (choice + migrate), js/audio.js (opts.pitch), js/data.js (skill weight
field, replace explicit hitstop calls). Milestone: M3 (DESIGN §16.1 item 1). 1.5 agent-days (0.75 core + loop fix,
0.25 settings/accessibility, 0.5 companion signals incl. pitch).

### Do NOT do
- No verlet / multi-part bodies, no sprite gibs, no ctx.rotate on sprites, no per-type death art commissions.
- No "dying" enemy state that keeps e.dead false: it stalls arena gates and triggers (F5). Use the corpse record.
- No additive hitstop, no global freeze per target in AoE, no slowmo on common kills, no camera zoom punch.
- Do not raise the global freeze above 6 steps for anything but a boss BREAK; touch players feel it as lag.
- Do not fork hitlag numbers per platform; the fixed step keeps them identical, keep it that way.
- Do not add a new js file for any of this (DESIGN §0: both html files, prefer extending).
- Do not touch Canvas 2D / renderer plans for this work (§26.4); measure p95 after R1+R2 on the phone first.
- Do not let hitlag Off also switch off number scaling, knockback or flash; those are the accessible power signals.
- Do not critique or iterate the boss bar from docs/boss.png; it is the old HUD.
