## 1. Verdict

The current opening is a competent tutorial corridor and a weak first chapter. It teaches the verbs, it fires every
trigger, and QA found zero deaths in the Vale. But it does not deliver the one thing this game is about. The hero wakes
at second 0 in a complete iron suit (Rusty Blade, Iron Cap, Iron Mail, Iron Boots) plus a free Origin ring, with eleven
bound skill slots on the HUD, standing next to a chest. The first chest then hands you a *second* weapon of a different
family and one more armour piece; the first drops of the game are two Rusty Blades, duplicates of the sword you already
hold (kbm-012). "You see what you wear" never fires because nothing ever changes on the hero that the player can see:
she is a knight at 0:00 and a slightly greener knight at 2:00.

Secondary problems: Pip, the guide and the keeper of the twist, has no body on screen (Pip is a dialogue speaker only;
the companion entity is §11.6 M7 "should"). The twist is seeded by exactly two lines (Elder: "The Stone knows her"; tablet:
"Four Wardens. One would not wake") that a player reads in a window with a layout bug (kbm-004: the marker text and the
trigger dialogue render on top of each other). The Vale measures ~2 minutes for the QA bot against a 5:30 plan in §16.2
and a "~6 min" claim in the vale.js header, because the pauses the plan counts on (fishing cast, Tome, bag tutorial) are
not actually in the route: fishing is deferred ("Fishing waits in the Pinewood"), the Tome is not at the Stone, the bag
never opens. And the beat order differs between three documents (§16.2: chest at 1:30 then fishing then Elder at 3:30;
vale.js header: dummies → Elder → chest; vale.js timing block: chest before dummies).

What works and must be kept: the look and tone (kbm-002 is a beautiful first frame), the trigger architecture, the Vale's
80×80 layout with dummies/Stone within 15 tiles and the chest at 10 (§25.3, verified by act1-route.js), the voice rules,
every existing STORY.md line, the garden ambush as the first real fight, the rift opening as the reward for it, and the
lost-lantern quest as the Vale's side quest.

## 2. Recommended opening: minute by minute

### Starting state
* **Location:** the Dreamer's bed (the start shrine, already named "The Dreamer's bed" in vale.js), on the Arrival knoll,
  camera on the sleeping hero, the cottage visible on the far hill. Pip is a small light over the bed.
* **Equipment:** **bare.** No weapon, no head, no chest, no boots, no ring. The hero wears the nightshirt/under-tunic the
  paper doll already draws when every slot is null (hero.js: "any slot may be null"). The HUD shows only LMB (a weak
  punch/shove, 1 damage, cannot kill a slime quickly) and nothing else; skill chips appear when a weapon is held.
* **Why bare, not kitted:** the core fantasy is gear-as-identity, and the only way to make the first weapon *felt* is for
  the hero to have been visibly empty-handed thirty seconds earlier. A Common→Uncommon swap of the same iron silhouette
  produces a 0.38-alpha green rim and nothing else (hero.js rarityFx). The silhouette change (hands empty → sword; tunic →
  mail) is free, already supported by the renderer, and is the strongest visual event the engine can produce in minute 1.
  "Immediate fun" is preserved because the first weapon arrives at 0:45, not at minute 5.
* **The Origin ring at minute 3:** not too much, but it must be *found*, not pre-equipped. Code today equips it at second 0
  (starterKit) and a second ring sits in the ledge chest, so the Vale has two rings and the first one is unearned.
  Recommendation: the Origin ring **is** the ring in the first chest (§19.4 "the first ring from the first Vale chest"),
  it is the first trinket noun at ~2:10, and the ledge chest loses its `loot:'ring'`. Note the Origin picker does not
  exist (items.js:350, origin follows the weapon's class); the recommendation below needs no picker.

### The question that pulls the player forward
Minute 1: "What is this place?" Minute 3: "Who is 'her'?" (the Stone's line). Minute 5: "What is screaming in the
Pinewood?" Minute 10: "Can I beat what is under there with what I have?" Each question is a bigger noun than the last.

## 3. How to seed the twist without spoiling it
STORY.md caps the twist at the lines it lists; do not add explanatory lines. Seed with placement and silence:
* The cottage is a *child's* room: one small bed, the Iron Cap on it, a lantern on the sill. No line. Players who return
  after Act IV will understand.
* The Stone's "her" is the only pronoun in the Vale that has no referent. Keep it exactly that way; do not add "Aerin".
* Pip's "Three days" line (new, 7 words) is the single new seed; it pays off "she is three days old".
* The lantern motif is already the seed: Pip is the last light of Aerin's lantern; the Vale quest is a lost lantern; the
  true ending runs through lanterns. The lost lantern should be found *in the cottage garden*, "still warm", not in a
  secret nook: a warm lantern no one has lit is the quiet question.
* Pip deflects once in the Vale: after the Stone's line, a second Pip line "Later." (1 word) fires in the same box,
  establishing the deflection pattern STORY.md uses at the Grotto and Caldera. (No Interact-on-Pip needed; the Pip light
  in change #12 is not interactable.)

## 4. Pip's role
Pip must have a body. A dialogue speaker with no sprite is a narrator, and the ending asks the player to grieve it.
Minimal version (S/M): a 3-px warm light with a bob, parented to the hero at an offset, no AI, drawn in the lights pass
(the engine already has 40-light budgets and cached light sprites). It leads: when an objective is set, Pip drifts 2
tiles toward it for 1 s, then returns. This replaces the sign at the start ("Training yard south-east") which is a
noun-cost with no warmth. The full companion (pickup, call-outs) stays M7.

## 5. How the opening sets up the §26.2 gear loop
In 0–10 the loop is seeded by exactly three things, in this order: (1) the first chest teaching that equipping changes
*look and numbers* in the same moment (bag auto-open on the item); (2) room 1's Uncommon at ~7:00 and then the elite at
~9:00 dropping a Rare weapon together with the key (§11.1 rules), so "elite = better gear" is learned before any boss;
(3) the Hollow door's "Recommended ilvl N · yours M" at 10:00. The Vale must not have a farm moment; it is the calm before. The elite pockets near Mossjaw
and Bramblehorn then have a learned meaning.

## 6. Tone
The dream is established by what is missing: no HUD at wake (fade the HUD in with each noun), no sign, and a quiet
first minute: AUDIO.md (authoritative for music) puts Aerin's Lullaby on the title, so the proposal is lullaby on the
title → only the wind-and-crickets ambient bed from wake → the Vale loop enters on the first swing. Confirm the handover
against AUDIO.md's engine plan before scheduling it. Pip's light is the only moving thing in the first frame. The art (kbm-002) already does the rest. Melancholy comes from the cottage, not from lines.

## 7. What the current opening does well / wrong / cut
**Well:** first frame; trigger architecture and QA coverage; the Vale geometry; the three-slime ambush as first fight;
the rift as the ambush reward; the lost-lantern quest reward; every STORY.md line.
**Wrong:** full kit + ring at 0:00; 11 HUD slots at 0:00; first drops are the starter weapon; Pip invisible; Elder text
double-render; three-verb dummy line; chest visible at wake while the objective points elsewhere; fishing deferred; no
Tome at the Stone; no bag auto-open; three conflicting beat orders; Vale 2 min vs 5:30 plan.
**Cut:** the arrival sign; the ledge chest's `loot:'ring'`; the starter chest's "try another weapon family" roll (a Knight
holding a bow at minute 2 with a Knight ring is a mismatch; family choice belongs to Pinewood drops); the "Fishing waits
in the Pinewood" line.

## 10. Conflicts with existing design decisions

1. **§19.4 "the Origin passive is a free starter ring's boon, so ring slot 1 means something at minute 3" vs. code
   equipping it at 0:00 and a second ring in the ledge chest.** Recommendation: keep the ring; move it into the first
   chest (~2:00); remove the ledge ring. Ring slot 1 then means something at minute 2 *and* was earned. Acquisition line
   "the first ring from the first Vale chest" already says this; the doc only needs the "starter" word softened.
2. **§17.1 "Starting kit" per Origin vs. starting bare.** Recommendation: the Origin decides *which* weapon stands in
   the grass at 0:45 and which armour family the Vale hands out (Knight: sword + Knight Plate line; Mystic: staff + Robe
   line, etc.), so the Origin is still a kit, found over 5 minutes. No picker exists; until it does, Knight is the default,
   exactly as code does now. Pip's per-Origin first line (§17.1) is unaffected.
3. **§16.2 timing vs. vale.js (and vale.js vs. itself).** §16.2 is labelled authoritative but predates §25.3; vale.js
   follows §25.3. Recommendation: keep §16.2's *principle* (one noun per minute) and rewrite its timings to the table
   above; edit the vale.js header and timing block to match. One order: wake → weapon → dummies → chest+fishing → Stone →
   cottage → ambush → rift.
4. **§16.2 "Uncommon weapon + Chipped gem" in the first chest vs. "every piece earned".** Recommendation: Common mail +
   ring + gem in the chest; the first *Uncommon* is Pinewood room 1's clear drop (~7:00) and the first *Rare* is the
   elite's weapon (~9:00), both exactly as §11.1 already rules. The first blue sparkle then coincides with the first key
   and the first "farm" lesson, which is what §26.2 wants the player to learn.
5. **§11.6 Pip companion = M7 "should" vs. Pip visible at 0:10.** Recommendation: split it. A parented light with no AI
   is a render-lane S/M and belongs in the slice; pickup/call-outs stay M7.
6. **§26.2 reverses "an under-geared hero must always win" vs. QA's Mossjaw/Bramblehorn 0/19.** Not an opening issue,
   but the opening is where the player learns that gear decides bosses. If the elite at 9:00 does not hand out a
   visible upgrade, the door's "Recommended ilvl" number at 10:00 is noise.
7. **§11.1 "room clear ≥ Uncommon" vs. the garden ambush dropping Common Iron Boots.** The garden is the only room in
   the game whose clear drop is Common. Recommendation: accept the exception and write it into §11.1 as "(Vale tutorial
   ambush: a fixed Common, the hero's first boots)". The Vale's level band [1,3] and `gloomslime.loot` pool need the
   same exception so no Uncommon Rusty Blade duplicate appears before the player holds an Uncommon of her own.
