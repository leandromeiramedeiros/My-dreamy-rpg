## 1.8 Gaps and overlaps the audit surfaced (the agenda for Round 2)
1. **Shatter vs frost**: shatter is listed as an element (`shatterDmg`, the sound, the Dawnblade implicit) but is a reaction
   on frost's ladder. Semantically it must be demoted to a *reaction damage type*; the key stays for save compatibility.
2. **Slash/pierce as "physical"**: icons and HUD collapse them to `physical`; resist rows and enemy archetypes (§5b armoured /
   amorphous / ethereal) split them. The split is doing real work in enemy design; it stays (ruled in Round 4).
3. **Nature = poison + heals + Sprint + Snare**: the tag means four things. Heals typed nature is a tagging accident with a
   consequence once statuses apply on hit (a self-heal cannot Poison, but `naturePower` would amplify Mend).
4. **Arcane = Gravity Well's pull + Blink + Mana Surge + Shadow Veil + Dreamveil's slow**: arcane is the "utility bucket".
   This collides head-on with a Wind "grouping" element (Round 2, CSD).
5. **Lightning has one skill**; its ring boon promises "Shock lasts 1 s longer" for a status that does not exist.
6. **Dreamveil** (AoE 35 % speed for 4 s) is a stronger Chill than frost's own Chill, typed arcane.
7. **Frost Nova** freezes for 2 s flat with no build-up; VFX B3 freezes for 1 s on tier 2. Both can't be true.
8. **Elite `frozen` mod is rare** and does nothing a frost enemy would do; `storm` is the best elite mod (it teaches
   Shock's arc before Shock exists).
9. **Bosses**: the §5b "immune only to their own element, never to statuses" rule vs B2's post-consume immunity windows.
10. **World**: nothing in the world reacts to an element. Brazier has no lit state; water cannot change; no trigger sees an
    element. Everything in the client's examples is new engine work, but small, because `openGate`, `hostileZone`, `use`
    blocks and chunked rasters already exist.

# Round 2. Proposals (each expert, independently)

## 2.1 Combat & Systems Designer: roles, Wind, the classics, the matrix

### 2.1.1 Principle
"One verb per element, readable in one hit, and every ladder ends in a thing the *other* elements want." The client's three
examples are the template: a tier-1 status you feel (burning, slowed, jolted), a tier-2 status you see (ablaze, frozen,
shocked), and a payoff that needs a second input (detonate, shatter, chain). Every element below follows that shape, physical
included, so the matrix is a grid and not a list of exceptions.

### 2.1.2 Role per element (the proposal; numbers are start values for balsim)
| Element | Role (one line) | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Payoff needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **fire** | explosive force + damage over time | **Burning** 3 s: 14 / 0.5 s | **Ablaze** 4 s: tick ×2 and **every fire hit on an Ablaze body splashes 30 % to 1 tile** (the "explosive force" is fire's shape, not a separate status) | stagger (Detonate), poison (Wildfire), holy (Purify), wind (Drift) |
| **frost** | control: slow → stop → break | **Chilled** 3 s: speed ×0.6, attack wind-up ×1.2 | **Frozen** 1 s (boss 0.25 s): no action; **any physical hit (slash / pierce / stagger ≥ 1) on a Frozen body Shatters** | physical (Shatter), lightning (Conduct) |

Stagger is the **physical ladder's third tier**: Bleeding/Rent and Pierced/Pinned fill the bar faster (+1 each on tier 2),
and a BREAK is where Rupture and Detonate live. That is how "earth / armour break" is covered without an earth element.

### 2.1.3 Wind, evaluated honestly
The client asked for Wind "focused on grouping". CSD's finding: **grouping already exists on the Staff** (Gravity Well,
`pull`, arcane, E slot, Act I). A second pull element would be a duplicate verb. What the game lacks is the *opposite* and
the *spread*: push, knock-up, scatter, carry. So the recommended Wind is **displacement in both directions plus spreading**:
* **Gust** (push a cone away), **Updraft** (lift a circle, they fall together: this *is* grouping, because Lifted bodies are
  drawn to the centre), **Vortex** (the Gravity Well tag re-housed: a wind zone that gathers).
* Its reaction, **Drift**, carries whatever tier-1 status the primary target has to everything the gust touches. For a fire
  build, Drift is Wildfire without needing Poison; for a frost build it is a Chill wave; for arcane it spreads Marks. Wind is
  thereby the element that makes **every other element want a second element** — the client's "reason to use more than one"
  without a chore, since Wind gives no damage of its own worth building around.
* Gravity Well's pull: CSD proposes it keeps arcane damage and gains the `vortex` **tag**; tags, not elements, describe
  shapes (§18.1). The Wind *element* owns Buffeted/Lifted/Drift; the vortex *shape* is shared.
* Bosses are never Lifted (fairness and animation cost); Buffeted on a boss is a 0.5-tile shove only on moves with `open > 1 s`.

### 2.1.6 Rules: stacking, bosses, immunity, caps, cascades
* **Build-up** (VFX B2 accepted as the mechanism, amended): every elemental hit adds `25 · w · proc · (1 + statusChance) ·
  (1 + elementAmp)` build in its element, with `w` the §27 weight and **`proc` the §18.6 proc coefficient** (basic 1.0,
  projectile 0.6, zone tick 0.3, reflected 0.3, summon 0.4, Echo 0.5). Without `proc`, a Rain of Arrows zone would Chill a
  room in one tick. Weak ×1.5, resist ×0.5, immune 0. Tiers at **50 / 100 / 150**. Decay 20/s after 1.5 s without a hit.
* **Bosses** take build ×0.25, elites ×0.5. Tier-2 durations on bosses as in the table. **Bosses are never immune to a
  status** (§5b stands): the post-consume window below is a *reaction cooldown* on that body, not a resistance.
* **Consumption**: a named reaction clears that ladder and starts a **4 s (boss 8 s) reaction cooldown** for that element's
  tier 2 on that body. Frozen / Lifted / Rooted / Dazzled cannot chain-lock.
* **Two ladders live per body** (the oldest is dropped) + the Wet flag + the stagger bar. The hero likewise.
* **Stacking**: Poison ×3, Marks ×5; everything else refreshes to max.
* **Cascade bound (§18.6 chain depth 2)**: a status applied *by a reaction* (Wildfire's spread, Drift's carry, Conduct's
  jump) is tagged `viaReaction` and **cannot itself fire a reaction for 1 s**, and its build counts ×0.5. Drift never carries a
  tier-2 status, never a `viaReaction` status, and carries to at most 6 bodies. Reaction damage stays under the 300 %/s cap.
* **Caps**: DoT per body ≤ 300 % weapon dmg/s; arcs 6 per 0.5 s per screen; Lifted at most 6 bodies at once; Shatter and
  Detonate AoEs do not re-trigger on the bodies they hit (the AoE is typed `shatter`, which is on no ladder).
* **Hero-side** (fairness): tier 2 is never a hard CC on the hero. Frozen → **Numb** (0.5 s root, dodge breaks it, i-frames
  kept); Lifted → a 1-tile knockback only; Rooted stays (Bramblehorn's root-grab is the existing mechanic, 1.5 s, dodge
  i-frames beat it); Dazzled → aim assist off for 1 s; Shocked → MP regen 0 for 2 s; Burning / Poisoned tick at 2 % max HP /
  0.5 s. Cleanse: potions with the Cleansing Sash, shrines, holy skills, Dreamveil.

## 2.3 Level & Puzzle Designer: the elemental world verbs
LPD: "Every verb must be an **object with a state**, hit by an **element receiver**, read by a **trigger condition**. Three
primitives, then everything the client listed is data." She mapped the client's two examples first.

### 2.3.1 The three primitives (engine)
1. **Receiver**: an object field `el: { accept: ['fire'], build: 50 }` registered in the spatial grid (`gridQuery objs`).
   One call site, `hitReceivers(x, y, r, el, w)`, runs in `hitArea`, `hitCone`, projectile explode and zone ticks (the same
   four places that call `damageEnemy`). A receiver accumulates build like an enemy ladder and flips `state` at its
   threshold. Receivers also accept **Pip's charge** and **Dream Oil** hits.
2. **State**: `o.state` ∈ the object's `states[]`; `setState(id, state)` is an action and a lasting effect replayed on
   respawn/settle exactly like `openGate` (game.js:1385-1412); saved in **`G.prog.state[map][id]`** (new key; `G.prog.obj`
   is taken by the objective log). Tolerant v4 addition: absent = default state. Each state names a sprite frame, a light,
   a collider flag, an optional tile patch.
3. **Condition**: trigger WHEN `object_state { id, state }` and WHEN `element_hit { x, y, r, element, count }`; THEN
   `setState { id, state }` and `tilePatch { id }`. With Lumina #16 the same is `if: state:torch_1 = lit and
   state:torch_2 = lit`.

### 2.3.3 Mapping onto the trigger / condition system, the generator and the editor
* **Triggers**: two new WHEN cards (`object_state`, `element_hit`) and two new THEN cards (`setState`, `tilePatch`) in
  editor.js `WHEN` / `ACTIONS` (editor.js:425-452), each with a `pick` field. With Lumina #16 the `if:` string gets
  `state:<id> = <state>` and `hits:<id> >= n` atoms.
* **Generator**: `gen.js` gains receiver objects in `OBJ` (sp/r rows, gen.js:140-158) and three secret-path kinds
  (`p.via = 'fire' | 'frost' | 'wind' | …`). **Reachability**: the audit (gen.js:1085-1125) learns `via`: a secret whose
  end is only reachable across a `via` patch is checked *with that patch applied*, and the audit fails if a **main-path**
  route needs any `via` (the gating rule, enforced by CI). Markers for verb puzzles carry `secretOnly` as today.
* **Editor**: the Objects tab shows `state` as a dropdown for stateful objects and a "preview state" toggle; the Logic tab
  previews `tilePatch` as a ghost overlay. ~1 d.
* **Height (2.5D)**: ice bridges sit at the bank's height; icefalls and vines are *climb pieces* (G3 root ladder: a 1-tile
  wide stair of h-steps) so they use the walkable rule, not a new movement; wind platforms are tile-set swaps at one height.
  Vantage points (G3) are where fog-blowing and windmill puzzles are *seen* before they are solved (the §29 rule "the boss
  is seen before it is reached" applies to puzzles).

### 2.3.4 Dungeon and secret rules, puzzle counts
1. **Never gate main progress on an element the player may not have.** Every main-path verb has a **world key within 20
   tiles**: a lit brazier (Pip carries fire), a rime crystal (Pip carries frost, Act III+), a storm conduit (lightning,
   Act II+), a dawn shrine (holy, Act II+), **or** the Elder Stone's Dream Oil of that element is sold from the act it is
   needed. Pip's charge is the universal key; skills are the fast way.
2. **Secrets may require a skill element** (1 of the 5 secrets per map, "the elemental secret"), and the Codex-style hint
   (Pip: "That ice looks thin to a flame") names the element.
3. **Puzzle count per big map: 3 verb puzzles** — one teaching (main path, world-keyed), one optional shortcut, one secret.
   Vault sections get at most one, arenas none (bosses use elements as mechanics, not puzzles).
4. **One verb per section is new**; a map never introduces two new verbs in one section.
5. **Nothing is a trap**: thawing ice pushes you to the bank; grass fire never blocks the only exit; wind platforms wait.
6. **Reset rule**: receivers reset on map re-entry unless their state is a lasting effect (lit torches, grown vines, broken
   seals persist; ice, fog, fire do not).

## 4.3 The interaction matrix, final
The 2.1.5 table is adopted as written, with the **"max 8 named reactions, all readable" rule**:
1. **Named reactions: exactly eight** — Shatter, Wildfire, Conduct, Overload, Purify, Rupture, Detonate, **Drift**.
   This supersedes §15.4's "seven, no more" by one, under the client's scope authorisation; the ceiling is now **eight**.
   A ninth requires cutting one.
2. **Readable** means: a unique input pair (no two reactions share an incoming element and a consumed status), a name pop in
   the reaction's colour, its own sfx (`rx_drift` to add), its own motif (Drift uses the carried element's sheet in a
   ring), and a bestiary "best reaction" line.
3. **Silent interactions: four** — Thaw, Quench, Flash-freeze, Dry. No pop, no sfx; a 6-particle puff of the ending status's
   colour. They may grow to six without a meeting; they never get names (D5).
4. **Cascade bound**: statuses applied by a reaction are `viaReaction` (1 s lockout, build ×0.5); chain depth 2 holds;
   reaction damage ≤ 300 % weapon dmg/s per body; the Shatter/Detonate AoE is typed `shatter` and starts no ladder.
5. **Two ladders + Wet + stagger per body; 4 s / 8 s reaction cooldown on tier 2 after a consume** (a cooldown, not an
   immunity: §5b "never immune to statuses" stands).
6. **Reaction share** stays 20-30 % of a reaction build's DPS, never above 40 % (§20.5); Drift's carried DoT counts in it.

## 4.8 Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Readability in a crowd** (8 ladders, Wet, stagger, elites) | 2 ladders per body, 2 glyphs per body on phone, Wet has no icon, the bestiary's "best reaction" line, the §15.8 element dot on the skill icon ("this will Shatter"); the crowd-8 / phone-crowd shots are acceptance |
| **Wind reads as "random knockback"** on phone | Buffeted is a 1-tile push *along the gust* (the cone is drawn), Lifted gathers (never scatters); bosses never move; Echo never applies wind |
| **Balance: Drift makes every DoT build a room-wipe** | carries tier 1 only, ×0.5 build, 1 s lockout, ≤ 6 bodies; the 300 %/s cap; balsim reaction-share red flag at 40 % |
| **Jolted interrupts trivialise casters** | ICD 1.5 s per body, only wind-ups ≤ 0.3 s; bosses flinch, never interrupt; healers' 4 s heal is unaffected |
| **Perf: tile re-raster on phone** | ≤ 4 chunks per verb, time-sliced through `groundTick`, river freezes behind a 0.4 s fade; ice patches ≤ 24 tiles; one grass fire per map; A7's p95 gate re-run with a burning ring in view |
| **Save compatibility** | no item fields; `G.prog.state` tolerant; no version bump; fixture round-trip in CI |
| **The gating rule is broken by a later level designer** | the generator audit fails any main route needing a `via`; the editor's problems check warns; CI red flag |
| **Scope creep inside M4b** (every map wants six puzzles) | the 3-per-map rule, 1 in the Vale, and the 24-min map cap: a puzzle that pushes a map over loses the puzzle, not a room |
| **Opal dilutes gem drops** (7 types, same drop rate = 14 % fewer Rubies) | Opal drops only from Act I's Hawk's Rest chest and from M4b on; gem drop weights by zone (the Grotto favours Sapphire/Topaz, the Caldera Ruby/Opal) |
| **Art collision** (15 objects + windmill frames in M4b while variants and enemies run) | 8 of 15 are skins; the art agent on enemies takes the 3 framed props in idle time; cut order: raft → windmill frames (static + spin fx) → fog bank (reuse fog tiles) |
| **Narrative: Wind has no Warden** | that is the point (the Dream's breath); Gale's tablet line and Pip's Hawk's Rest line seed it; the Drifting Isles pay it off |
