# Credits

Code and techniques adapted from other projects. One line per lift.

## Lumina (gitlab.com/stubborn-hug/lumina)

Lumina has no licence file yet; the client accepted the risk and authorised the lifts (docs/meetings/2026-10-20-lumina.md §0.4, §5).
The lines below were re-written for Canvas 2D rather than pasted.

* Adapted from Lumina by stubborn-hug (gitlab.com/stubborn-hug/lumina): src/game/ResolutionGovernor.js → js/main.js `GOV` / `governorStep` / `governorSettle`. The rules are kept: median of 5 samples, step down after 4 slow medians and up after 20 fast ones, a 3 s cooldown. The changes are DPR rungs of 0.125 from 0.5 to 1.0, CPU frame time as the timing source, and the 'low' quality tier below the lowest rung.
* Adapted from Lumina by stubborn-hug (gitlab.com/stubborn-hug/lumina): src/engine/lighting/LightPool.js → js/render.js `lightPool` / `lightCost`. The rules are kept: −2 cost for lights already lit, a re-rank every 0.2 s, and a 0.35 s fade when a light changes owner, inside fixed slots. The screen-space ranking and the per-frame light tracking are ours.
* Adapted from Lumina by stubborn-hug (gitlab.com/stubborn-hug/lumina): the emitter-only bloom (Lumina's emitter / FX glow, e.g. src/engine/fx/FxQuads.js, src/engine/world/props/Flame.js; their COMBAT-15/16 rule that VFX colours sit at the bloom threshold) → js/render.js `bloomEmitters` / `applyBloom`.
* Adapted from Lumina by stubborn-hug (gitlab.com/stubborn-hug/lumina): the fog clear zone around the player (a `destination-out` radial hole, cf. src/engine/lighting/Sky.js) → js/render.js `drawFogClear`.
* Adapted from Lumina by stubborn-hug (gitlab.com/stubborn-hug/lumina): src/engine/world/props/Trees.js `buildTree` / `buildBroadleaf` (crown leaf clusters on Fibonacci points of a flattened ellipsoid, plus a crown top and inner clusters; the occlusion term that darkens the interior and the underside) → js/art.js `canopyTree` (§29 G2). The clusters become three 2D pixel leaf masses with a rim light on every cluster. The trunk, roots, pines and freeze path are ours.
* Adapted from Lumina by stubborn-hug (gitlab.com/stubborn-hug/lumina): src/game/GroundDetail.js `groundDetailSteps` (the scatter grammar: an fBm clump mask at 0.23 per tile, more tufts in clumps, 25 % pale, flowers dense on flower tiles, ferns 55 % at the forest border and 5 % in the open, ferns ringing tree roots, sparse tufts on path edges, pebbles elsewhere) → js/maps.js `decalsAt` / `drawDecals`, remapped to our tile chars and baked into ground chunks and column tops. The 16 decal kinds (js/art.js `buildDecalSet`) are ours.
* Adapted from Lumina by stubborn-hug (gitlab.com/stubborn-hug/lumina): src/engine/world/props/Wind.js (the travelling gust `0.55 + 0.45·sin(0.43t − dot(pos, windDir)·k)` and the two-frequency sway `sin(1.21t + φ)·0.62 + sin(2.37t + 1.73φ)·0.38` with a position-hashed phase) → js/render.js `gustFrame`. k is retuned to 0.25 per tile, and the output picks a −1 / 0 / +1 px canopy frame.
* Adapted from Lumina by stubborn-hug (gitlab.com/stubborn-hug/lumina): src/engine/pixel/FxSprites.js (`createFxAtlas` grey-level painting with binary alpha, `framePen`, `stamp`, `packFrames`, the `arc` crescent and the `slash` frame parameters) → js/elfx.js `makeGrey` / `framePen` / `stamp` / `packFrames` / `PAINT.slashArc`, the element sheets and the §31 atlas. Lumina's levels map onto our 4-step ramps, and only the arc's leading tip is the core step. The tint cache (LUT and source-in backends), the Bayer dissolve rows (util.js `BAYER`, the same table as Lumina's `lpBayer4`), the fire burst and frost hit painters, and the motifs are ours.
* Adapted from Lumina by stubborn-hug (gitlab.com/stubborn-hug/lumina): src/engine/fx/Particles.js (the closed-form motion of `drift` / `emit` / `fall`: emit's exponential drag `p0 + v0·(1 − e^−ka)/k`, fall's sway, "life ends at spawn height"; the `PARTICLE_PRESETS` / `DEFAULTS` row shape) → js/elfx.js `MODELS` / `pos` / `spawn` and the registry's model fields. The GLSL becomes JS on the CPU, and units are converted at 1 tile = 16 px. The rise, jitter, orbit and bounce models, the hashed stateless emitters and the 48-slot `makePool` ring (the UIP prototype) are ours.
* Adapted from Lumina by stubborn-hug (gitlab.com/stubborn-hug/lumina): src/game/combat/CombatFx.js (the `EFFECTS` data table: frame, mode, lift, loops, scale, back; and its COMBAT-15/16 lesson that big white frames bloom into glare) → js/elfx.js `EFFECTS` / `FX_FRAMES` / `fxDraw` and rule R5 (each motif has at most one core pixel; the arc core is its tip only).
